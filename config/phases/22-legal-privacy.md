## Goal
Cover licensing, consent, and data-handling obligations.

## Checklist
- Dataset license
- Open-source licenses
- API terms of service
- User consent
- Privacy policy if necessary
- Terms and conditions
- Cookie handling
- Personal-data storage
- Data retention
- User-data deletion
- Copyright checks
